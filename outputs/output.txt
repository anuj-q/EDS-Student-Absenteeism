Decision tree as if-then rules (fitted on all 94 responses):
Likert / impact answers are coded 0=lowest ... 4=highest.
A split at 2.5 therefore means 'Neutral or below' vs 'Agree / Positive or above'.
One-hot features are 0 or 1. The 'value' numbers in the PNG are class-weighted;
the student counts printed here are the raw numbers.

if Boring lectures <= 2.50 (i.e. Neutral or below  vs  Agree or Strongly agree):
    if Reason: Attendance not strictly enforced <= 0.50 (0 = no, 1 = yes for this one-hot option):
        → predict Not frequent  (leaf has 18 students: 18 not frequent, 0 frequent skippers)
    else  # Reason: Attendance not strictly enforced > 0.50:
        → predict Frequent skipper  (leaf has 6 students: 4 not frequent, 2 frequent skippers)
else  # Boring lectures > 2.50:
    if Poor sleep (mornings) <= 2.50 (i.e. Neutral or below  vs  Agree or Strongly agree):
        if Perceived impact <= 2.50 (i.e. Neutral or more negative  vs  Positive or Very positive):
            → predict Not frequent  (leaf has 22 students: 17 not frequent, 5 frequent skippers)
        else  # Perceived impact > 2.50:
            → predict Not frequent  (leaf has 10 students: 10 not frequent, 0 frequent skippers)
    else  # Poor sleep (mornings) > 2.50:
        if Would improve attendance: Stricter attendance rules <= 0.50 (0 = no, 1 = yes for this one-hot option):
            → predict Frequent skipper  (leaf has 30 students: 15 not frequent, 15 frequent skippers)
        else  # Would improve attendance: Stricter attendance rules > 0.50:
            → predict Not frequent  (leaf has 8 students: 7 not frequent, 1 frequent skippers)
